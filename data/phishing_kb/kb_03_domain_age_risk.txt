WHOIS Domain Age Evaluation Rules: Newly registered domains (NRDs) that are less than 14 days old account for over 70% of active phishing infrastructure. High-risk indicators include: domain registered within 30 days, registration via high-volume privacy registrars (e.g., NameCheap, Porkbun, CheapName), and hidden WHOIS owner details for domains claiming to represent regulated financial or government entities.

Sri Lanka CERT Alert - Fake Online Banking Portals: Threat actors frequently register lookalike domains targeting Sri Lankan commercial banks (e.g., Commercial Bank, Hatton National Bank, Sampath Bank, People's Bank). Common patterns include adding hyphens, spelling variations (e.g., combank-online-portal.lk or sampath-verify-login.com), or substituting letters with numbers (hnb-onl1ne.net). Any portal asking for a 2FA OTP, SMS verification code, or debit card PIN directly on a login page is a flagged phishing signature.

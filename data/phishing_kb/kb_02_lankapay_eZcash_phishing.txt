Sri Lanka Mobile Wallet & CEFTS Payment Fraud: Phishing campaigns frequently impersonate national payment networks like LankaPay, eZ Cash, and Dialog/Mobitel bill gateways. Fraudulent websites notify victims of "failed transactions" or "pending cash refunds" and direct them to input secret PINs, card CVVs, or OTPs. Valid LankaPay processing pages never ask users to re-enter primary account numbers or PIN codes to receive funds.

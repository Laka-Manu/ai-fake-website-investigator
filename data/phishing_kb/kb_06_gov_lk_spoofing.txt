Official .gov.lk vs. Fake Public Sector Domains: Official Sri Lankan government services exclusively use .gov.lk or .nic.lk top-level domains managed by LK Domain Registry. Fraudulent government portals (e.g., passport appointment systems, inland revenue tax refunds, fuel pass schemes) often use commercial TLDs like .com, .net, .xyz, or .top. Any site claiming to represent a Sri Lankan ministry or department without a .gov.lk extension should be marked SUSPICIOUS.

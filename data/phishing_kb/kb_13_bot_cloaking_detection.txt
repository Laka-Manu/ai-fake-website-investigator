Cloaking & Geofencing Countermeasures: Advanced phishing kits use IP geofencing (allowing only Sri Lankan residential IP ranges) and user-agent filtering to block security scanners/headless browsers (e.g., returning 404 or redirecting to Google.com when accessed by a Python/Playwright user-agent). Agents should flag sites that return dramatically different HTTP status codes or DOM trees when probed with mobile vs. desktop headers.

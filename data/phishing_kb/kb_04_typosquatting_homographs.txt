Typosquatting & Punycode Exploitation: Phishing operators use Internationalized Domain Names (IDNs) to replace Latin characters with identical-looking Cyrillic or Greek characters (e.g., replacing 'a' with 'а' to create pаypal.com, which resolves to xn--pypal-43a.com). If the raw domain starts with xn--, or contains string distances (Levenstein distance = 1) from major financial brands, elevate the threat score to HIGH.

HTML Form Inspection & Actions: Phishing sites display specific form behaviors: (1) Form actions pointing to third-party scripts, free PHP webhosts, or Webhook URLs (e.g., discord.com/api/webhooks, telegram.org/bot); (2) Forms requesting sensitive inputs (password, SSN, NIC number, credit card) on unencrypted HTTP connections; (3) Forms that do not perform client-side format checks and accept fake/blank logins.

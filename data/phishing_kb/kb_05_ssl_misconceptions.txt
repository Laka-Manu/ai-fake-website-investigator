Free Certificate Authority (CA) Misuse in Phishing: The presence of a valid HTTPS green padlock does NOT equal trust. Over 80% of phishing sites now use free, automated DV (Domain Validated) certificates issued by Let's Encrypt, ZeroSSL, or Cloudflare. High-risk signals include: DV certificates issued within 48 hours of domain registration, multi-domain SAN certificates mixing unrelated financial brand keywords, or short validity windows (<90 days).
